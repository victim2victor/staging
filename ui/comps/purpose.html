Our Purpose Statement

Our purpose is to restore our collective identity by being the repairer of broken walls and the restorer of streets with dwellings, as Isaiah 58:12 instructs. By doing so, we aim to rebuild our sense of self-worth, purpose, and dignity as individuals and as a community, recognizing and embracing the unique qualities that make us who we are. Through this process, we will strive to reclaim our authentic selves, build meaningful connections with others, and positively impact the world around us.

Isaiah 58:12
